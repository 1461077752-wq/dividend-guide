## How SCHD Picks Its Stocks

SCHD tracks the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index. The index screens large U.S. companies on four factors:

1. **Cash flow to total debt** — financial health
2. **Return on equity** — profitability
3. **Dividend yield** — current income
5. **Dividend growth rate** — (5-year)

The result: 100 dividend-paying companies that score well on quality and yield — a blend of Dividend Aristocrats and other strong payers. Top sector weightings typically include financials, healthcare, consumer staples, and industrials (per index provider methodology).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does SCHD pay dividends?
Quarterly. SCHD typically pays in March, June, September, and December. The exact ex-dividend and payment dates are published by Schwab each quarter.

### Is SCHD a good dividend ETF?
For most investors, yes. It combines a strong yield (3.4%), quality stock selection, low fees (0.06%), and a history of distribution growth. It's a core holding for many dividend-focused portfolios.

### How does SCHD's dividend compare to VIG or VYM?
SCHD offers a higher yield than VIG (1.8%) with similar fee. VYM has slightly lower yield (2.9%) but broader holdings. SCHD's advantage is its quality screen and dividend growth focus.

### Are SCHD dividends qualified?
Most of SCHD's distributions are qualified dividends taxed at capital gains rates (0/15/20%), making it tax-efficient. Check your 1099-DIV for the qualified percentage each year.

### Can I reinvest SCHD dividends automatically?
Yes. All major brokerages (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard) offer free DRIP enrollment for SCHD. See our [DRIP guide](/articles/drip-compounding-guide/) for the full setup walkthrough.

### What is SCHD's yield on cost?
Yield on cost depends on your purchase price. An investor buying SCHD at $52 in 2019 earning $2.73/year now has a ~5.2% yield on cost, versus the 3.4% current market yield.

### Does SCHD ever cut its dividend?
It hasn't since launch (2011). Distributions grew every year. However, no dividend is guaranteed — a severe market event or index methodology change could alter payouts.

### What stocks does SCHD hold?
Approximately 100 companies selected by the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index. Historically heavy in financials, healthcare, consumer staples, and industrials. Full holdings are published by Schwab quarterly.
