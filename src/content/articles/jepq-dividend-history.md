## Why JEPQ's Payouts Move: Volatility Is the Fuel

JEPQ's monthly distribution isn't a fixed coupon — it's the option premium the fund collects from selling covered calls (via ELNs) on its Nasdaq-100 portfolio. That means payouts track **Nasdaq volatility**, not company earnings:

| Period | Distribution | What Drove It |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Jul 2022 | $0.340 (low) | Calm market, thin premiums |
| Nov 2022 | $0.681 (peak) | High volatility, CPI surprises |
| Feb 2024 | $0.342 (low) | Quiet start to 2024 |
| Sep 2024 | $0.557 | Volatility pickup |
| Jun 2025 | $0.621 | Elevated volatility |
| Aug 2026 | $0.705 (record) | Market volatility spike in July 2026 |

*Monthly amounts per [StockAnalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/etf/jepq/dividend/); event context from [JPMorgan fund disclosures](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/adv/products/jpmorgan-nasdaq-equity-premium-income-etf-etf-shares-46654q203).*

The key insight: **when markets get turbulent, JEPQ pays more.** Higher implied volatility makes options (and the ELN premium) more expensive, so the fund's income rises exactly when investors are most anxious. That's why 2025 — a year with tariff shocks and sector rotation — produced JEPQ's largest annual distribution.

## JEPQ Dividend History Questions

### What is JEPQ's dividend history?
JEPQ has paid monthly since June 2022: ~$3.85 total in 2022 (partial year), $5.00 in 2023, $5.44 in 2024, and $6.12 in 2025. Annual totals have risen every full year, and monthly payouts range from ~$0.34 to a record ~$0.70.

### How much does JEPQ pay monthly?
Recently about **$0.44–$0.71 per share** per month, with the August 2026 distribution at a record $0.705. On a $60,000 position at current prices, that's roughly $480–$560 per month. Payments arrive monthly, typically with an ex-date on the first business day and payment within days.

### What is JEPQ's current yield?
Approximately **11%** on a trailing-12-month basis as of August 2026, based on ~$6.52/share of trailing distributions and a ~$58 share price.

### Is JEPQ's dividend sustainable?
JEPQ's distributions are option premium income, not company earnings, so they're variable — but they've grown every year because the fund's ELN-covered-call strategy has kept generating premium. Unlike single-stock covered-call funds, the price has held up, which makes the income more durable.

### Are JEPQ dividends qualified?
Mostly no. JEPQ's distributions are largely ordinary income from options premium, making it tax-inefficient in taxable accounts. Per [IRS Form 1099-DIV rules](https://www.irs.gov), the character is reported annually — most investors hold JEPQ in an IRA or 401(k).

### Why did JEPQ's dividend go up in 2025?
Higher market volatility in 2025 (tariff shocks, sector rotation) drove up option premiums, which flows straight into higher monthly distributions. JEPQ pays more exactly when markets are turbulent.

### Did JEPQ ever cut its dividend?
No single payment has ever been cut to zero, and annual totals have risen every full year. Individual monthly payouts do fluctuate — the low months were quiet-volatility periods, not distress.

### Is JEPQ better than QQQI for dividends?
JEPQ has the longer track record, lower fee (0.35% vs 0.68%), and proven price stability, but a lower yield (~11% vs ~14%) and worse tax treatment. QQQI's history is still young. Many income investors hold both.
