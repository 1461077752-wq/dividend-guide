SCHD (Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF) has raised its annual dividend in **every calendar year since its 2011 launch** — 13 consecutive year-over-year increases through 2025. Its split-adjusted annual dividend grew from roughly **$0.27 per share in 2012 to $1.05 in 2025**, an average increase of about **10.9% per year**. That unbroken growth record, more than the yield itself, is what makes SCHD the most popular dividend-growth ETF in the market.

## How the 2024 Stock Split Changed SCHD's Dividend Math

In October 2024, SCHD executed a **3-for-1 share split** — shareholders got two extra shares for every one they held, and the price dropped to one-third. Per [Schwab's fund page](https://www.schwabassetmanagement.com/products/schd), shares began trading post-split on October 11, 2024.

This matters for dividend history because it changes the *numbers* but not the *income*:

| Before Split | After Split |
|-------------|------------|
| 1 share × $0.66 Q4 2024 dividend | 3 shares × $0.22 Q4 2024 dividend |
| Same total income | Same total income |
| Pre-2024 dividends: $0.81/yr (2012) | Split-adjusted: $0.27/yr (2012) |

**The key insight:** if you held SCHD before October 2024, your annual income didn't change at the split — you just receive the same dollar amount across three times as many shares. Dividend history tables on most financial sites now show split-adjusted numbers, which is what this article uses.

## SCHD Dividend History Questions

### Has SCHD ever cut its dividend?
No. SCHD has paid a higher annual dividend every calendar year since its 2011 launch — 13 consecutive increases through 2025. No single quarter has been cut to zero, though a few individual quarters were flat versus the prior year.

### What is SCHD's dividend history?
Annual dividend grew from ~$0.27/share (2012, split-adjusted) to $1.05/share (2025). Growth averaged ~10.9% per year, with the fastest years in 2019-2020 and the slowest in 2023.

### Why did SCHD's dividend drop after October 2024?
It didn't — that's the 3-for-1 split. Before the split, the quarterly dividend was ~$0.66; after, it's ~$0.22-0.26 per share. Multiplied by three shares, the income is the same. Financial sites show split-adjusted history so the growth line stays continuous.

### Does SCHD pay monthly or quarterly?
Quarterly. Payments typically arrive in March, June, September, and December. Some brokerages offer a "dividend drip" that turns each quarterly payment into fractional shares.

### How much does SCHD pay in dividends now?
Approximately $1.05 per share over the trailing 12 months as of August 2026, which at the current ~$33.50 share price is a ~3.1% yield. On [our dividend yield analysis](/articles/schd-dividend-yield/), the yield has ranged between roughly 2.9% and 4.0% over the years.

### What is SCHD's dividend growth rate?
About 10.9% average per year from 2013 through 2025. The 2025 figure was +5.2%; 2019's was the fastest at +19.8%. See the full year-by-year table above.

### Are SCHD dividends qualified?
Mostly yes — the majority of SCHD distributions are qualified dividends taxed at long-term capital gains rates (0/15/20%). Check your 1099-DIV each year for the exact qualified percentage.

### Is SCHD's dividend sustainable?
The record suggests yes: SCHD tracks the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index, which screens for financial health (cash flow-to-debt and return on equity) before yield. That quality screen is why the fund never cut through 2020 or 2022.
