## Ex-Date, Record Date and Pay Date Are Different Jobs

JEPQ's calendar uses the same day for its ex-date and record date in 2026. The pay date follows two to five calendar days later.

### Ex-dividend date

If you buy JEPQ on or after the ex-dividend date, you generally do not receive that month's distribution. A purchase must settle under the market's rules, so investors should verify broker treatment rather than trading solely from a calendar.

### Record date

This is the date J.P. Morgan checks its shareholder records. In the official 2026 schedule, JEPQ's record date matches the ex-date.

### Payment date

This is when the fund sends the distribution. Your broker may post the cash later in the day or on the next business day. A short posting delay does not necessarily mean the payment was missed.

## Why JEPQ's Monthly Amount Changes

JEPQ seeks current income while maintaining prospects for capital appreciation. It owns a large-cap equity portfolio and uses an options overlay through equity-linked notes. The amount available for distribution can change with option premiums, portfolio dividends, expenses and tax accounting.

That is why a payout-ratio figure designed for an operating company can mislead when applied to JEPQ. The fund's prospectus states that distributions may be ordinary income or capital gains, and J.P. Morgan's calendar says extra income or capital-gain distributions may occur during the year. Check the final Form 1099-DIV for tax classification.

## JEPQ Dividend Date Questions

### When is JEPQ's next dividend date?

The next scheduled ex-date and record date is September 1, 2026. The scheduled payment date is September 3, 2026. The amount had not been declared as of August 24.

### Does JEPQ pay dividends monthly?

Yes. JEPQ is listed in J.P. Morgan's monthly distributing ETF group. Monthly frequency does not mean the amount is fixed.

### What time does the JEPQ dividend arrive?

The sponsor provides a payment date, not an intraday posting time. Brokers process fund payments on their own schedules, so cash may appear later on the pay date or the next business day.

### Can I buy JEPQ on the ex-dividend date and receive the payment?

Generally no. A buyer on or after the ex-date normally does not receive that distribution. The market price also tends to adjust for the distribution, so buying solely to capture the payment does not create free income.

### Is the JEPQ dividend qualified?

JEPQ distributions can contain ordinary income or capital gains, and the mix may differ by year. Rely on the fund's year-end tax information and your Form 1099-DIV. The [IRS explains dividend categories](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc404), but personal tax treatment requires individual advice.

### What happens if JEPQ changes a date?

J.P. Morgan marks every scheduled date as subject to change. Recheck the sponsor's distribution notice before trading or budgeting around an upcoming payment.
