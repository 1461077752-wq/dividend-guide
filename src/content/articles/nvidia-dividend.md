NVIDIA pays a quarterly dividend. In May 2026, the company raised it from **$0.01 to $0.25 per share**, making the indicated annual NVIDIA dividend **$1.00 per share**. That was a 25-fold increase, but NVDA remains primarily a growth stock: at a hypothetical $220 share price, the forward yield is only about **0.45%**.

| Item | 2026 figure | Source / calculation |
|---|---:|---|

| First record date at new rate | June 4, 2026 | NVIDIA Q1 FY2027 release |
| First payment at new rate | June 26, 2026 | NVIDIA Q1 FY2027 release |
| Previous quarterly rate | $0.01 per share | NVIDIA FY2027 Q1 [Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000052/nvda-20260426.htm) |
| Forward yield at $220 | 0.45% | $1.00 ÷ $220; illustrative price |

The board must declare every future payment. NVIDIA states in its Form 10-Q that future dividends remain subject to the board's judgment, so the $1.00 annual figure is an indicated run rate, not a contractual promise.

## What Changed in 2026

NVIDIA announced the new $0.25 quarterly rate alongside its results for the quarter ended April 26, 2026. The same release reported **$81.6 billion of quarterly revenue**, **$58.3 billion of GAAP net income**, and about **$20.0 billion returned to shareholders** through repurchases and cash dividends. Those figures come directly from the [company's May 20 release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-first-quarter-fiscal-2027).

The scale matters. At roughly 24.2 billion shares outstanding, a $0.25 payment costs about **$6.1 billion per quarter**, or about **$24.2 billion annually** if the share count and rate remain unchanged. That estimate is our calculation, not company guidance. It compares with the old $0.01 rate, which cost roughly $243 million in the April 2026 quarter according to NVIDIA's Form 10-Q.

### A large percentage increase can still produce a small yield

The jump from $0.01 to $0.25 sounds enormous because the old base was tiny. Yield puts the number in context:

## NVIDIA Dividend History, Adjusted for Stock Splits

NVIDIA began paying a cash dividend in 2012. Comparing old payments without adjusting for splits is misleading because the company completed a 4-for-1 split in 2021 and a 10-for-1 split in 2024.

| Period | Dividend as announced at the time | Split-adjusted equivalent today | What changed |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2012 launch | $0.075 quarterly | about $0.001875 | First regular dividend |
| 2018–2020 | $0.16 quarterly | about $0.004 | Rate held through the pandemic period |
| After 2021 split | $0.04 quarterly | $0.004 after the 2024 split | Same economics after 4-for-1 split |
| June 2024 onward | $0.01 quarterly | $0.01 | 10-for-1 split-adjusted rate |

The split conversion follows NVIDIA's official [2024 stock split FAQ](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_downloads/2024/06/nvidia-2024-stock-split_faq_investors.pdf). Historical figures are rounded to make different share counts comparable.

## Is NVIDIA a Dividend Stock Now?

NVIDIA is a dividend-paying stock, but it is not a conventional high-income stock. Three facts explain the distinction:

- The indicated $1.00 annual payment yields well under 1% at the price examples above.
- NVIDIA approved an additional **$80.0 billion** share-repurchase authorization in May 2026, much larger than one year of dividends at the new run rate, according to the company release.
- Business results, AI infrastructure demand and valuation still drive the investment case far more than quarterly cash income.

## NVIDIA Dividend Questions

### Does NVIDIA pay a dividend?

Yes. NVIDIA pays quarterly cash dividends. The company raised the quarterly rate to $0.25 per share in May 2026, implying $1.00 annually if that rate continues for four quarters.

### How much is the NVIDIA dividend in 2026?

The board-approved quarterly amount announced in May 2026 was $0.25 per share. The first payment at that rate was scheduled for June 26, 2026 to holders of record on June 4.

### What is NVIDIA's dividend yield?

Yield changes with NVDA's price. A $1.00 annual dividend equals 0.56% at $180, 0.45% at $220 and 0.38% at $260. Use the current market price when calculating your own figure.

### Why did NVIDIA increase its dividend so much?

NVIDIA did not give a single-purpose explanation, but it announced the increase alongside record Q1 FY2027 revenue, $50.3 billion of quarterly operating cash flow and a larger buyback authorization. The inference is that stronger cash generation gave the board room to return more cash while continuing heavy investment.

### When does NVIDIA pay dividends?

NVIDIA has generally paid quarterly. Exact record and payment dates are declared with each quarterly result; they should be verified in the latest NVIDIA investor release rather than projected from a fixed calendar.

### Is the NVIDIA dividend qualified?

For many U.S. taxpayers, ordinary corporate dividends may qualify for the lower qualified-dividend tax rate when holding-period and account rules are met. Tax treatment depends on the investor, so verify the entry on Form 1099-DIV and consult the [IRS dividend guidance](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc404).
